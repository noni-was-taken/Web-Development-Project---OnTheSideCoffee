Welcome

At OnTheSide, we believe in the simple joys of good coffee, delicious food, and warm conversations. Our cozy space is the perfect spot to unwind, catch up with friends, or get some work done while enjoying a freshly brewed cup of coffee and homemade treats.

From rich espresso drinks to refreshing iced teas, and from hearty breakfasts to sweet pastries, everything we serve is made with care and quality ingredients. Whether you're stopping by for your morning pick-me-up or an afternoon snack, we're here to make your day a little brighter.

Come in, take a seat, and stay awhile--we're glad you're here.
